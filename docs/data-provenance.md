## Satellite imagery and supporting datasets

For each scene or data product, record the provider, product ID, acquisition time, spatial resolution, bands/variables, cloud information if provided, CRS, license/terms, query parameters, and local path. Keep credentials out of this file.

Do not mark a dataset as acquired until the files have been downloaded and checked.
